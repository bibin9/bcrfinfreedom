# BCR FIRE — User Manual

A 5-minute guide for anyone — **no finance background needed.**

> **Live app:** https://finfreedom-advisor.pages.dev
> **In-app help:** tap the **Help** button (top-right of any page) for this manual.

---

## 1. What is this app?

**BCR FIRE** helps you figure out one simple question:

> *"When can I stop working and live off my savings?"*

It does the maths for you using your country's real numbers — inflation,
investment returns, average living costs — so you see how much money you
really need and how to get there.

> **Educational only.** Numbers shown are estimates, not financial advice.
> Talk to a registered advisor before investing.

---

## 2. What does "FIRE" mean?

**FIRE = Financial Independence, Retire Early.**
It's a global movement built on one simple rule:

> When your savings are **25× your yearly spending**, you can live off them
> for life. That's the **"4% rule"**.

### Example, very simple:

- You spend **₹14 lakhs / year** on everything (rent, food, school, fuel).
- 25 × ₹14 L = **₹3.5 crores**. That's your FIRE number.
- Hit ₹3.5 Cr → take out 4% (₹14 L) every year → your savings still grow on the other 96%.

The app figures out **your** FIRE number based on where you live and your
lifestyle, then shows you how many years it'll take.

## 3. Quick start — 3 steps

### Step 1 · Tell us about you
Pick your country, age, and monthly income.
You can change these any time by hitting **Edit** at the top of the dashboard.

### Step 2 · Set your lifestyle in Fine-tune
On the right side of the dashboard:
- Pick **Single** or **Family of 4** — the app uses your country's average for that household.
- If you spend more (or less), type the real number in *"Your annual expenses"*.

### Step 3 · Read your FIRE plan
Open the **Freedom** tab. The big orange box is your **FI Ratio** — how
close you are to FIRE today. Below it are FIRE tiers, charts, and a
year-by-year plan.

## 5. The Fine-tune panel (right side of dashboard)

Every slider updates the numbers live:

- **Savings rate** — what % of salary you save each month.
  **Most powerful lever.** Doubling it roughly halves your years to FIRE.
- **Target freedom age** — pick when you want to stop working.
  Younger = bigger SIP needed.
- **Household** — Single or Family of 4. Changes the country expense benchmark.
- **Your annual expenses** *(optional)* — type your real number if you know
  it. Best accuracy.
- **Current invested corpus** — what you already have invested today.
  Counts toward your FI Ratio.

## 6. FIRE tiers in plain English

| Tier | Multiple | Plain English |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 **LeanFIRE** | 15× expenses | Frugal retirement. Smaller home, public transport, cooking at home. Cheapest exit. |
| 🔥 **FIRE** | 25× expenses | The classic. Live your current lifestyle indefinitely on 4% withdrawals. |
| 🏆 **FatFIRE** | 33× expenses | Comfortable. Travel, eat out, hobbies. Uses safer 3% withdrawals. |
| 📈 **CoastFIRE** | varies | The amount you need today that — even if you stop saving — compounds into your full FIRE number by retirement age. Once you hit Coast, you can take a lower-paying job you love. |

## 7. Key numbers, explained

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **FI Ratio** | Your current invested money ÷ today's FIRE number, as a %. Hits 100% = you're free. |
| **FIRE number** | Total money you need to retire. Always 25× your yearly spending (after inflation if it's a future target). |
| **Required SIP** | How much you need to invest every month to hit FIRE by your target age. |
| **Years to FIRE** | How many years until your wealth crosses your FIRE number at your current savings. |
| **Expected return** | What your portfolio earns per year on average. India ~11%, UAE ~8%, US ~8.5%. |
| **Inflation** | How fast prices rise per year. Things cost more in 20 years — the app builds that in. |
| **SIP** | Systematic Investment Plan. A fixed amount you invest every month, automatically. |

## 8. Pro tips for accurate numbers

- **Be honest about expenses.** Track 3 months of spending and put the
  average into *"Your annual expenses"*. The FIRE number is only as good as
  this input.
- **Pick the country you'll retire in**, not just where you live now.
  An NRI in UAE retiring in India should pick India for the FIRE number.
- **Update yearly**, not daily. After every salary raise or big life change
  (marriage, kids, house).
- **Automate the SIP on payday.** Money you don't see is money you don't
  spend. People who automate save 3× better.
- **Don't panic-sell during dips.** Compounding rewards consistency.
  The Paths tab shows what happens to people who do.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

### Is my data sent anywhere?
**No.** Everything lives in your browser's local storage. Nothing leaves
your phone. You can hit *Start over* in the header to wipe it.

### What if I'm an expat saving in one country, retiring in another?
Right now, **pick the country you'll retire in** — that drives your FIRE
number. Use the NRI checkbox if you're an Indian non-resident. A proper
dual-country mode is on the roadmap.

### Why does my FIRE number look so big?
**Inflation.** ₹14 L today becomes ₹36 L in 18 years at 5.5% inflation. The
app sizes your corpus for what life will actually cost *then*, not now.

### What's a "good" savings rate?
Whatever you can sustain. The classic FIRE community targets 50%+. Most
retail savers do 10–20%. Even moving from 15% → 25% knocks 5–8 years off
your timeline.

### Should I include my house in "current corpus"?
**No.** Only **liquid investments** — mutual funds, stocks, ETFs, fixed
deposits, gold ETFs. Your primary home doesn't generate withdrawal income.

### The app's expected return seems high — is that realistic?
It's a 20–30 year nominal average for your country's main index. Short
periods will be lower or higher. The 4% rule is built to survive bad decades.
